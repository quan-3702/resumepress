📄 How to use ResumePress

ResumePress helps you write a resume and cover letter, check them, and keep track of the jobs you apply for. Everything stays on this computer – no account, no subscription.

Want to see an example first? ☰ More → Open a sample resume.

1. Fill in your resume

Work down the sections on the left: contact details, summary, experience, education and skills. The preview on the right updates as you type, and everything saves automatically. Click a section name to open or close it. Use ↑ ↓ to change the order of sections or entries, and 👁 to hide a section. Add more sections at the bottom – projects, certifications, languages, awards, volunteering or your own custom section.

For each job, write 3–5 lines about what you achieved, one per line. Start with an action verb and add a number where you can: “Cut onboarding time from 21 to 12 days”.

Stuck? Click 💡 Ideas next to a job's bullet points for example lines for 18 common jobs and a list of strong action verbs, or 💡 Examples next to the summary.

2. Choose a look

Click 🎨 Template to pick one of 8 designs, then choose a color, font, paper size (Letter or A4) and text size. Templates marked “ATS-friendly” use a simple one-column layout that application tracking systems read easily. The red dashed line in the preview shows where a new page starts.

3. Check your score

The Score button checks your resume for common problems: missing contact details, weak phrases like “responsible for”, bullets without numbers, “I/me/my”, placeholders you forgot to replace, too few skills and length. Fix the red and yellow items and watch the score go up.

4. Print or save as PDF

Click 🖨 Print / PDF (or press Ctrl+P). In the print window choose Save as PDF or Microsoft Print to PDF to get a PDF file to upload or email. Turn on Background graphics if colors are missing. For online application forms, use ☰ More → Export plain text and paste it in.

5. Match a job ad (Pro)

Open 🎯 Job match, paste the job ad and click Compare. ResumePress shows which important words from the ad your resume already has and which are missing. Click a missing keyword to add it to your skills – but only add skills you really have. Make a tailored copy creates a separate version of your resume for that job.

6. Cover letter (Pro)

Open ✉️ Cover letter, fill in who it goes to and click Write a first draft from my resume. Replace the [brackets] with your own words. The letter uses the same template and colors as your resume.

7. Track your applications

In 📋 Applications, add every job you apply for with its status, dates, link and notes. Follow-ups that are due are shown in red. The free version tracks 5 jobs; Pro is unlimited.

8. Several resumes and backups

Use the resume list at the top to switch between resumes. ☰ More → Duplicate this resume makes a copy for another job (Pro). Save a backup now and then with ☰ More → Back up everything; restore it on another PC with Restore a backup. Export to Word (Pro) gives you a .doc file for final edits in Word.
