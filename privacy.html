ResumePress – Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

ResumePress does not collect, send or sell any personal information. Your resumes, cover letters, photos, job descriptions and application list are stored only on your own device, in the app's local storage. Job descriptions you paste into Job match are analyzed on your device only.

ResumePress has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Data leaves your device only when you print, save a PDF, export a Word or text file, or save a backup file yourself.

Purchases of ResumePress Pro are handled by the Microsoft Store. ResumePress only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it never sees your payment details.

You can delete your data at any time by deleting a resume (☰ More → Delete this resume) or by uninstalling the app.
